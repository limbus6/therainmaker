# Roadmap validation packet

This is the human observation layer for the engineering gates in `roadmap.md`.
Automated tests prove determinism, causal state, save isolation, migrations, and
full playthrough safety; they cannot honestly prove that a player feels tension,
remembers a character, or chooses to return tomorrow.

## One-session protocol

Use a fresh browser profile or choose a new mandate. Do not use QA checkpoints
for timing or recall observations.

1. Start a Flagship mandate with one build. Record time to the first meaningful
   choice and first visible consequence. Targets: under 30 and 60 seconds.
2. Mark every action as decision, information, or administration. No ordinary
   stretch should contain more than two consecutive no-choice advances.
3. At the end of Phases 5, 6, and 7, stop for at least five minutes. On return,
   verify that Previously / What matters now / What happens next is enough to
   resume without reopening several screens.
4. Record whether the Golden Slice (Phases 5–7) takes roughly 15–20 minutes,
   whether the Ricardo/Vektor chain is recalled, and whether the offer reveal is
   described as a highlight rather than an interruption.
5. Use the build's active ability once. Ask the player to name the ability, its
   cost, and the later consequence. The Results Board must show Decisions, Risk
   profile, Relationship, and whether the ability was Used or Held.
6. Complete the board, offer, signing, and closing ceremonies. Verify each is
   fast, skippable, readable at 390 px, and that skipping does not alter the
   mechanical outcome.

## Cross-run protocol

1. Complete one Headwinds and one Hot Market mandate. Each must contain five
   stages and should land near 15–20 minutes without omitted work appearing as
   process evidence.
2. Start a second mandate within five minutes of Results. Confirm the tombstone,
   career reputation, record provenance, and Beacon rule remain understandable.
3. Compare two different builds. The players should describe different actions,
   not just different passive numbers.
4. Build a long career toward 50 tombstones. Failed deals must remain legible and
   the Deal Shelf must still feel collectible rather than administrative.
5. Ask, without prompting, for the rival's name and one remembered encounter.

## Daily and challenge protocol

1. On two devices, open the same UTC Daily during the same content season. The
   mandate, build, seed, starting bonus, buyer field, and event order must match.
2. Finish once, replay, and verify that only the first result is ranked.
3. Copy an RM1 challenge to another player. They must see the declared
   configuration before launch and receive the same starting state/event order.
4. Confirm Daily and Challenge attempts never alter Career or Beacon.
5. Repeat on the next UTC date and record whether players return unprompted.

## Reporting

Record seed, content version, build, mandate, device width, elapsed times, skipped
ceremonies, and short answers to the recall questions. A behavioral criterion is
passed only from observed player behavior; absence of a bug is not a substitute.
